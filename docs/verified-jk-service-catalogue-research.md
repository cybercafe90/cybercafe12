## Publication and import rules

1. Treat the 29 entries above as a research shortlist only. Verify each service is currently offered for Srinagar, whether online submission is available, and whether the cited portal remains the correct destination before activating it.
2. Preserve the existing generic service names until a reviewed mapping confirms whether an item is a duplicate, a more precise variant, or a distinct application.
3. Do not mark every service as `official_url_verified=true` just because a portal homepage is official. Verification must cover the specific service-to-portal relationship.
4. Do not invent eligibility, required documents, service fees, guaranteed timelines, or government approvals. Leave unknown fields blank or state that the authority determines them.
5. Keep public assistance prices hidden, as requested by the business owner.
6. Do not imply government affiliation, government authorization, or direct access to government systems.
7. Do not import into production until migration drift is reconciled, a backup/restore plan is reviewed, and separate production database approval is recorded.
