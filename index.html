Digital service assistance, made simpler.

BABA'S CYBER CAFE is an independent digital service assistance centre in Srinagar, Jammu & Kashmir. We help people understand online application steps, prepare documents and make enquiries about certificates, insurance, transport and other public services.

Explore our service categories

Browse the main areas where our team can provide independent application and documentation assistance. Actual availability, eligibility, official requirements and decisions are controlled by the relevant department, insurer or service provider.

Insurance services

Motor, two-wheeler, four-wheeler, health, life and other insurance enquiries, renewal support and policy documentation assistance.

Revenue certificates

Domicile, income, category and residence certificate application assistance, plus land-record information support.

Birth and death registration enquiries, certificate-related documentation and municipal application assistance.

How our assistance works

  1. Choose a service category or contact us with your question.
  2. We explain the information and documents that may be needed for your enquiry.
  3. Where available, we assist with the relevant online application process.
  4. Official eligibility checks, fees, approvals and final decisions remain with the responsible authority or provider.
