RTO and Transport Services

Application and document-preparation assistance for driving licences, vehicle registration and related transport services.

Services and enquiries

  • Learner licence assistance
  • Driving licence application assistance
  • Driving licence renewal
  • Vehicle registration and RC-related enquiries
  • NOC and permit assistance
  • Traffic challan information assistance

How we can help

Contact us with your requirement. We can explain the information and documents that may be needed and provide independent assistance with available online application steps. Please do not send passwords, PINs or one-time passcodes.

Important: The transport department controls official applications, eligibility, fees, tests and decisions. BABA'S CYBER CAFE is an independent assistance business and is not a government department or issuing authority. No approval or processing time is guaranteed.
